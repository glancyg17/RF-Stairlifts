A working funnel, with nothing live behind it

Two entry pages feed one six-question quiz. Every answer is scored against the draft lead spec and routed. Leads are emailed to you once the site is live, and a copy stays in your browser, so you can try the whole journey and then see what the buyer would receive.

What is real and what is not

Real: the quiz logic, UK phone and postcode checks, the spam trap, duplicate blocking, click-ID capture, the tier and stream rules, consent logging, and the installer's company number, Which? rating and two job photos, all taken from public pages on 25 October 2026. Not real: sending leads to the installer, our own phone number and company details, install counts and anything else marked in yellow brackets.
