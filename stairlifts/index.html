Been told it can't be done?

Sometimes a different rail design, seat or parking position solves what one firm walked away from. Sometimes nothing will, and we'll say so. These are the situations we see most.

Steps that fan round a corner

Winding steps need a rail built to the exact turn. Some firms decline them; specialists take them on.

Narrow stairs

A slimmer seat or a folding design can sometimes work. The measurements decide.

Steep stairs

Steepness limits which lifts are suitable. We'll check what's realistic for yours.

Short at the top or bottom

When the stairs stop short of a landing or doorway, where the lift parks matters as much as the rail.

A door opens onto the stairs

A door at either end can sometimes be worked around with the right track and seat position.

Heavier or taller users

Weight and size limits vary by model, and some stairs suit a perching seat better.
